The Great Oak Siege

Big skeletons. Three branch paths. One very important doorstep.

  1. Build: choose a trap and tap a glowing spot.
  2. Stack: Log Drops send skeletons onto the branch below. Place brambles under them.
  3. Salvage: tap loose bones to send your chipmunk.

Skeletons follow the arrows and climb down at the ends. Only enemies reaching the bottom doorstep damage the Oak. All traps fire automatically.

FIELD NOTES

Let gravity help.

Root Snare: holds a skeleton, resets in 11 seconds.

Log Drop: opens a hatch and knocks one skeleton down a level without damage. Resets in 8 seconds. Build directly above brambles!

Brambles: automatically hit nearby skeletons on their branch. Reset in 5 seconds. Bone Brutes need two hits.

Uncollected bones make passing skeletons stronger. Tap bones to send the crew; nearby enemies make it retreat. Supplies deliver 20 materials every 15 seconds.

Tap a selected trap card again to cancel building. Remove gives a full refund before the wave, half during play.
